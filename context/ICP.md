# ICP.md — Ideal Customer Profile

## The buyer
Typically a **business owner, founder, or senior manager** who has been **tasked with
understanding and adopting AI** — the person accountable for figuring out what AI means
for their organization.

## Segments (in focus)
1. **Luxury companies** (Italy)
2. **American companies operating in Italy** — where my bilingual/bicultural bridge is
   especially valuable
3. **Smaller Italian companies**
4. **Non-profits** that could genuinely benefit from learning about AI
5. Broadly, **any organization willing to invest** at my rates

## Geography
Companies across Italy where I have connections — **Milan, Lombardia, Puglia, Genova,
Valtellina**, and other regions.

## The trigger (why they come to me)
- They know they **should** be using AI but find it **scary or overwhelming**.
- They want to **improve performance, save time, and cut cost** but lack a clear path.
- They need someone who can both **advise and implement**, in **English or Italian**.

## Qualification — who to pursue first
- **Ability and willingness to pay** — hourly at **$800/hr**, or a **$2,500 starter
  retainer + a share of improvements**.
- **Where I can make a real, positive contribution** — including mission-driven orgs
  and non-profits with genuine upside.
- **Referral potential** — clients likely to generate word of mouth.

## Company size
Broad — from **boutique/luxury houses** to **small and mid-size Italian firms** and
**US subsidiaries** in Italy. Sweet spot: organizations small enough to move fast and
where a senior advisor can drive real change.

## Not a fit (for now)
- Buyers unwilling to invest at professional rates.
- Engagements that are pure theory with no path to implementation.
